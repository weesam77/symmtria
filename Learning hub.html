Garment Care & Stack-Up

Step-by-step handling for data reliability & fabric longevity

wash
1

Detach Core Hub Pod

Required Before Wash

Locate the central magnetic locking latch between the cups. Gently press the lower release latch and lift the pod up and outward. Never immerse the Core Pod in liquids.

touch_app Haptic click confirms complete release
2

Textile Garment Washing

Gentle / Hand

Hand-wash in cool water (≤30°C) with enzyme-free mild detergent, or place inside the included protective mesh wash pouch on a gentle delicate machine cycle.

air Dry Flat Only: Tumble-drying or direct heat disrupts conductive silver micro-yarns.
3

Inspect Capacitive Electrodes

BP-03 Inserts

Check the hydro-dry contact pads inside the inner lining. If residue from body lotion or mineral deposits appears, wipe gently with an alcohol prep swab before wearing.

4

Recharging & Dock Storage

Magnetic Dock

Place the Core Pod onto the cradle dock. An amber breathing LED indicates active charging; solid teal indicates full 100% capacity (good for 36 continuous wear hours).
