Diagnostic Boundaries & Guardrails

Permitted investigational scope under IDE authorization

check Permitted Investigational Uses
  • Longitudinal trend correlation comparing unilateral physiological drift vs. contralateral mirror quadrant.
  • Sub-surface hemodynamic tissue tracking via dual-wavelength continuous wave NIRS array (760nm / 850nm).
  • Multi-frequency bioimpedance spectroscopy (5kHz - 500kHz) for Cole-Cole extracellular matrix modeling.
  • Investigator research data logging in observational breast tissue remodeling trials.
block Strictly Prohibited Operations
  • Zero Autonomous Diagnosis: Never use as a standalone substitute for Full-Field Digital Mammography (FFDM), Automated Breast Ultrasound (ABUS), or MRI.
  • No Direct Interventional Changes: Surgical, biopsy, or chemotherapeutic decisions cannot be directed solely by SmartBra telemetry.
  • Emergency Triage: Garment is not configured or cleared as an acute cardiovascular monitor or emergency alarm device.
